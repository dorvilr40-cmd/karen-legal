Privacy Policy – Karen Analytics

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Who we are

Karen Analytics ("the Service") is an internal content analytics tool operated by Ritchy ("we", "us"). Contact: dorvilr36@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

When a TikTok account is connected, and only with its permission, we collect:

  • Basic profile information: display name, avatar and account identifier (scope user.info.basic)
  • Account statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts (scope user.info.stats)
  • Public videos and their statistics: title, description, publish date, views, likes, comments and shares (scope video.list)
  • The access tokens that TikTok issues to keep the connection active

We do not collect passwords, private messages, or any data from TikTok users other than the connected account.

3. How we use the data

We use this data only to analyze the performance of our own content and to improve future videos. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, except when required by law, or with service providers that help run the Service (for example hosting or AI analysis tools) under confidentiality obligations.

5. Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored securely and are only accessible to the operator. We take reasonable measures to protect them against unauthorized access.

6. Retention

We keep the data as long as the account stays connected and as long as it is useful for content analysis. When access is revoked, we delete the stored tokens and, on request, the related data.

7. Your choices and rights

You can revoke the Service's access at any time from your TikTok account settings (Security and permissions > Apps and services). You can also ask us to access or delete your data by writing to dorvilr36@gmail.com.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
